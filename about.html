OUR HISTORY

Rooted in learning, ready for tomorrow

Greenfield Model School was founded with a simple vision: to make meaningful, quality education accessible to children in a supportive environment. Over the years, our school community has grown through the dedication of teachers, students and families.

We continue to build on that foundation with modern teaching methods, co-curricular activities and a strong commitment to student wellbeing.
